AxionSky: Flying Game

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 6, 2026

AxionSky: Flying Game operates entirely locally on your device without collecting, transmitting, or storing any personal data or requiring registration.

Real-time sensor data (accelerometer and gyroscope) is processed in memory solely for flight controls and is never saved or shared.

The application does not use analytics, does not track users, does not display ads, and does not share data with third parties.
